About this study
This GTSIO-520-H detailed-cylinder study supports free inspection, guided lessons and local self-checks over the same 720-degree operating state.
Mechanism positions are profile-checked. Illustrative timing, flow paths and teaching colours are labelled rather than presented as measured engineering data.
