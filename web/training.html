What this shows

Only the piston, rod and crank move. Valves remain closed. This demonstrates the mechanism, not the complete operating cycle. Use Section view to see inside.

Operating cycleThe four-stroke cycle, in colour

Round particles move with the cycle and pause when you pause. Use View gas inside to reveal the chamber and port paths.

What this shows

Cyan: incoming fuel–air charge · blue: compression · orange: combustion/power · grey: exhaust. Moving streams connect the port mouths, valve openings and chamber. Solid parts hide particles behind them; Section view reveals the internal cues.

Illustrative motion and ideal stroke timing; not measured temperature, pressure or CFD. The port-to-chamber paths are schematic connections through CAD landmarks, not validated fluid trajectories. The particle region is not the complete chamber volume. Reference: GTSIO-520 manual A-3-3 / A-3-4, induction and continuous port injection.

What this shows

Filled cut faces show the material crossed by the section; real cavities stay open. Transparent surrounding parts omit the fill for clarity. This is a display section, not a CAD measurement. Use the component list to select a cut face.

About this study
This is a GTSIO-520-H detailed-cylinder teaching study. Explore supports free inspection; Learn and Check yourself add guided prompts and local self-checks over the same model and 720-degree operation state.
Mechanism positions are profile-checked. Valve timing, gas paths and coloured cycle cues are labelled where illustrative rather than treated as measured engineering data.
No learner identity, answers or progress are transmitted or persist by default.
