# Langley / Manly-Balzer 1903 radial: reconstruction study

Status (7 October 2026): **research complete; CAD spec written for all 358 parts; the rest-pose build is valid and the interference audit is clean (see `build-notes.md`).** Scope agreed with the user: engine proper plus flywheels and shaft stubs; end product an Explore card; reference is the 1911 Memoir drawings. The sister study is `cad-studies/wright-1903/revision-2/`; the aim is a side-by-side comparison of the two engines that were contemporaries in 1903 (a rugged horizontal four-cylinder inline against a light five-cylinder water-cooled radial).

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `research.md` | Identity, sources actually studied, **corrections to the commissioning brief**, construction and operating understanding, dimension hierarchy, readiness decision |
| `operating-motion.md` | Motion dossier (every ratio, stroke, lift, phase with a status), cause-and-effect chains, closure checks, packaging record |
| `inventory.json` | 45 components, 358 planned part IDs (the CAD spec builds exactly these), every line of Manly's weight table covered, 9 mechanism paths, conflicts and gaps (generated by `python -m cad_pipeline.langley_research`) |
| `research-readiness.json` | Result of the research gate (`cad_pipeline/research_gate.py`) and the weight-table coverage |
| `build-notes.md` | How to rebuild, mass against the weight table, what the audits found and how it was settled, what is still open |
| `mass-check.json` | Model mass per weight-table line (output of `scripts/langley_mass_check.py`) |
| `source-manifest.json` | Sources, access date, SHA-256 of the local copies, what could not be reached |

Local source copies are under `build/langley-research/` (ignored): the 1911 Memoir PDF and OCR text from the Internet Archive, and the Annals of Flight 6 PDF supplied by the user. They are not committed.

Checks that need only the standard library: `python3 scripts/test_langley_research.py` (displacement, power, weight table with its 65 g misprint, cam and firing-order closure, ignition ratios, shoe clearance bound, inventory completeness).
