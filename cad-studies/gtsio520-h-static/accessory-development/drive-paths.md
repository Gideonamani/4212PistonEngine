# Internal drive paths for the accessory-drive lesson

This is a reading guide to the current static candidate, not an operating-animation approval. Gear counts and packaging are calculated estimates unless separately identified as source dimensions. Positive/negative signs below use the model's common shaft-axis convention; they are not OEM clockwise labels.

| Path | Current relationship | Interface that makes the path physical |
|---|---|---|
| Crank to compound idler | 30/42 tooth ratio, opposite rotation | Pressed idler bush around a stationary, flanged support pin; separate attached accessory wheel |
| Idler to each magneto | Overall 1.5 times crank speed, same sign | Shaftgear journal, long forward-projecting bush, rear support adapter and simplified front coupling |
| Crank to cam | 30/60, opposite rotation, half crank speed | Timing gears and attached cam cluster |
| Cam cluster to replacement fuel drive | Estimated 24/24 pair; overall +0.5 crank speed | Separate matched estimated spline coupling, selected H replacement gasket and .75in exposed mounting studs |
| Cam to pressure pump | Internal spline; driver follows cam at -0.5 crank speed | Shaft passes through the fitted housing journal; separate driven gear rotates on a fixed spindle |
| Pressure shaft to scavenge pump | Keyed driver follows the same shaft | Separate gear chamber, driver key and driven bush; scavenge gears are not the tachometer bevel pair |
| Starter shaftgear to crank | External 24/30 pair; crank = -0.8 times this shaftgear speed | Supported shaftgear and one simplified stationary starter/adapter shell; motor-to-shaft worm/clutch behaviour is deferred |

The second idler wheel is present but its H-specific downstream route is unresolved. Do not teach it as a completed vacuum drive. Governor and tachometer bevel branches, and the alternator face-drive branch, remain absent from this candidate.

A gear ratio alone does not establish a working drive. In the lesson, trace torque through each coupling or key, radial load into journals and stationary supports, and axial retention separately. A shaft that clears every neighbouring solid can still be unsupported or disconnected. The magneto correction demonstrates why installation text and the direction of a bush projection must be checked alongside an exploded drawing.

For the governor branch, `governor-fit-planning.json` retains a source-based 22.2377mm case bore and a calculated 22.17674mm journal. The tested 55/34 bevel-method fixture matches the rounded H speed ratio, but its counts, module, location and installed supports are still estimates or pending. Do not present that fixture as an installed engine component.
