## Additional face-gear software-method lead

The original [FCGear crown-gear implementation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/looooo/freecad.gears/master/freecad/gears/crowngear.py), inspected2026-10-14, starts with an annular blank and cuts rotated lofted tools whose profiles vary across the radial band. Its preview mode leaves the teeth uncut. This is a candidate construction technique to test; it does not prove a compatible Continental face gear. No source code was incorporated, addon installed or face-gear geometry accepted. Retain a versioned source/fixture record before adopting a derived method.
