# Systems R2 construction and gate sequence

Use the existing realism worktree. Run CAD modules with FreeCAD1.1.1's Python,
with `scripts` on `PYTHONPATH`. Use one heavy engine/face/native/render stage
at a time. Check current workflow files before launching a job; do not duplicate
an existing process. No animation, Drive upload or student release is included.

The source chain ends in `gtsio_alternator_installation.py` followed by
`gtsio_oil_galleries.py`. Its1400-part input is the saved systems-r1 construction;
the candidate contains1440 parts. Do not regenerate a live specification during
a job that binds its hash. Native donors for the face pair and pressure receiver
have independent immutable specification/inventory snapshots.

`prepare_systems_r2_build.py` first binds the completed1400-part saved/direct
construction agreements and native reopening. This is explicitly construction
evidence: the independent engine pair audit may still be running. It adopts1388
unchanged direct parts, appends verified prefixes for both cases and the
reduction hub, adopts35 alternator parts and the pressure receiver, and builds
eight revised inserts plus four closures. The complete face ring stays absent
until its own native gate passes. `--finish` then imports the checked full face
pair and assembles the complete current direct geometry.

`audit_oil_galleries.py` checks actual current solids: twelve guide junctions at
zero, middle and full lift;25 direct groove-opening samples per junction; selected
3mm guide walls;1.5mm trunk/end stock; and one occupied pressure-domain volume
joining both trunks, front connection, four main films and four cam films.
Closure tool sockets and unresolved internal crankshaft oil space are outside
that diagnostic domain. They are never filled by adding fictional physical stock.

Its optional `--reuse-verified` requires the same actual direct BREP digests or
saved native archive, raw specification, gallery plan, audit source hash and
FreeCAD/OCC identity. A changed source rejects that reuse. The saved-native
version is a different geometry source and must be checked independently.

`run_systems_r2.py` is one finite sequential job, not a recurring monitor. It
waits for systems-r1's native, bearing-support and installed-drive gates, then:

1. Builds and checks the complete60-slot face pair, including saved hole edits,
   STEP,24 pitch samples and a wrong-phase negative control.
2. Completes direct construction, verifies/reuses current gallery results, and
   checks every changed part against its actual current bound-meeting neighbors.
3. Extends the native1400-part archive. Dependency-verified unchanged histories
   and eligible prefixes stay live;37 checked independent donor histories can
   be transferred, with all source hashes bound to checkpoint state. The twelve
   remaining insert/closure histories are built normally.
4. Reopens and checks STEP, fitted interfaces, independent direct agreement,
   every current exact neighboring pair, bearing support, installed drives and
   whole-case gallery/guide stock and occupied fluid space.
5. Renders the actual complete saved geometry, exports the complete static GLB,
   audits its actual meshes, and only then writes the integrated review.

The first failure stops later stages. Progress and logs are under
`build/gtsio520-systems-r2/integration-workflow.json` and `*-workflow.log`.
Construction checkpoints remain incomplete packages. Resume native work only
with the same source/specification/donor manifest. A completed but failed native
package needs a new revision after diagnosis; do not overwrite it to hide a failure.

`record_systems_checkpoint.py` retains scoped JSON evidence and method learning
in this source directory. It does not promote incomplete native/export results.
The35-part alternator review is local; its assembled29-part GLB excludes the
face-ring hardware until complete installation has passed. The prior1400-part
construction also has an explicit known finding: inherited open guide walls,
repaired in the1440-part candidate. Neither local report is full-engine acceptance.
