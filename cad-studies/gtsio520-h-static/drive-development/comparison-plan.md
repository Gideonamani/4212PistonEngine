# Visible comparison plan

Original X-30045A scan and full legends control the comparison. Inspect the same parts and state; the exploded perspective is used for feature construction, not pixel registration. Do not assign a silhouette score without isolated masks and a fitted, held-out camera calibration.

- A-4-20, PDF38: compare the propeller shaft's integral gear, ground journals, flange, quill spline ends, retaining plate/ring, separate transfer tube/sleeves, oil seal and thrust halves. `propeller-plan.json` isolates the main propeller subassembly; `open-drive-plan.json` shows its connections to the crank and cam. Loose parts in the source are assembled in the candidate, so account for occlusion before calling a feature missing.
- A-4-8 and A-4-19, PDF25/37: compare the lower cam axis, two timing gears, separately mounted cluster and shared shaft-flange attachment holes. Absolute tooth indexing is a calculated packing pose; source timing marks do not establish the estimated lobe law or operating phase.
- B-2 longitudinal section, PDF97: derive a native half-section with positive X camera direction and vertical Y up, placing the front Z direction left as in the drawing. Compare bearing support, quill coupling, thrust stack and axial oil-transfer path. Record an estimated, unregistered axial layout rather than treating the schematic limits chart as a dimensioned manufacturing section.
- A-4-42, PDF49: compare flange-hole presence and the shaft/gear shoulder sequence. The selected D-family attachment circle is documented for that interface family; exact H serial applicability and outer flange/pilot dimensions remain unresolved.
- A-4-4, PDF23 top image only: compare the fitted front case and complete candidate exterior. The K image below must not silently supply H accessory or induction geometry. Missing alternator/governor/induction/exhaust/ignition components are deferred systems, not evidence that the reviewed internal drive fit is correct or incorrect.

Use fixed neutral orthographic plans for repeatable before/after diagnosis. They are currently qualitative cameras, not registered source perspectives. Record each image, saved scene and plan hash after rendering. Inspect the exterior for silhouette/occlusion, the exposed drive for attachment/topology, and the section for negative space and interfaces. Classify each finding by observation, deduction or estimate, explain its likely cause, name the next native operation and affected mating surfaces. Keep geometry acceptance separate from the final image-matcher conclusion.
