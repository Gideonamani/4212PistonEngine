## Construction deductions

- Two aluminium castings join along the vertical centre plane. Map this to X=0; odd cylinders occupy +X/right, even cylinders -X/left. The left decks are forward, following the existing 44 mm offset and manual assembly numbering. Include hollow walls, main-bearing webs, cam tunnel, six open cylinder pilots, mount pads and separate sump, not a solid decorative shell.
- Four main journals receive split steel-backed lined inserts. Table 6 crankcase bearing-seat midpoint is 2.8165 in = 71.5391 mm. Main-journal midpoint is 66.6623 mm. B-2 row39 gives 0.001-0.004 in diametral clearance; select 0.0025 in = 0.0635 mm. Shell width leaves the R4 root blends clear. Final press fit and layered material details are simplified.
- Six third-order counterweights attach at cheeks 2,5,8 with two pins each, plates and rings. Hardened bushings occur in the blades and weights. Table11 gives pin diameter .4902-.4906 in; select .4904 in = 12.45616 mm. Cheek bushing ID .756 in and weight-bushing selected .755 in produce two radial race clearances. In the outward loaded static pose, the roller touches the outside of the blade race and inside of the weight race: pin displacement = blade radial clearance; weight displacement = sum of both clearances. This is a geometric contact pose, not a dynamic or third-order tuning validation.
- Weight forks straddle 8.4 mm blades. Select 0.4 mm total fork gap; derive fork side walls, bushing lengths and closed outer bridge. B-2 row52 gives .001-.023 in pin end clearance; select midpoint .012 in = .3048 mm. Retaining plates close the pin ends; rings sit in body grooves. Estimates cover crescent contours, axial mass width and retaining hardware sizes.
- An oil sump is a separate open casting with a mounting flange, gasket, drain and suction connection. Oil return openings remain physically open. Do not claim complete internal oil routing until the pump, gallery and cylinder returns are integrated.
