Reviewed 2026-10-08 using `image-matcher`. Candidate: `build/gtsio520-static`, model `gtsio520-h-static-engine-v1`. The presentation manifest records candidate CAD/GLB hashes, plate cameras and part identities; source lineage is recorded in foundation-research.md. Source views are A-4-18 PDF35–36, A-4-20 PDF38 and A-4-30 PDF43. Reviewed plates: overview, crankcase, dampers, open-case and supported-core.

This is a **feature-presence and construction review**, not registered silhouette measurement. The source illustrations and candidate camera have different perspectives; no IoU or authenticity percentage is reported. The review does not claim source-calibrated dimensions from that camera.

Conclusion: **needs another iteration**. The foundation has significantly better assembly construction and checked guest/host boundaries; source resemblance is incomplete until the front drive housing, valve train and exterior systems are added and compared. No hidden gallery or casting-wall accuracy claim is made.

## Retained lessons

The source guide blade can remain thin while its surrounding cheek needs more axial material to house a forked mass. Enlarging the blade along with the cheek would lose that design relationship. Native race tangency and separate end-gap calculations are more informative than a visually centred loose pin. Full-engine packaging also changes estimated axial pitch: damper/rod/head interfaces must share one layout, and the earlier R4 core remains archived rather than silently reinterpreted as a finished engine.
