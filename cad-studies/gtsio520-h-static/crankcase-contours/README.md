## Reusable approach for other engines

The current revision-by-revision receipt is `session-progress.json`. The user
has authorized cylinder-bank dimensional rework to fit documented H hardware
while retaining the internal drive backbone; see `bank-rework-decision.md`.
The calculated111.325mm trial deck half-width depends on estimated flange,
washer, nut and tip sizes and is not an OEM case dimension. H1B IPC rows and
inspected attachment graphics are recorded separately, with legacy/service
configuration differences retained. ContourR4 timed out without completed
case geometry. R5 is fixture development, not an accepted engine package.

1. Establish the variant and coordinate frame, then identify machined reference
   faces, shaft axes and attachment patterns before choosing an outer shell.
2. Separate four constraints: fitted interfaces, necessary supporting/sealing
   stock, moving clearance space, and intentionally open return/service paths.
   A collision check covers only one of these.
3. Distinguish cast material from separate pressed, bolted and gasketed parts.
   Trace their installation sequence and load/oil relationships from the source.
4. Compare the construction recipe with the drawing before changing curves.
   A repeated prism, an excessive cavity cut or a missing attachment can explain
   a poor resemblance better than an arbitrary fillet radius.
5. Test unfamiliar topology on one representative bay or nose sector. Save,
   reopen, edit a real control, compare independent geometry and inspect STEP.
6. Keep source dimensions, engineering deductions and selected estimates
   separate. Transfer the reasoning and method; never copy one engine's
   estimated spacings, wall sizes or bolt counts into another.
7. Reuse verified construction and audit results only with their controlling
   inputs, actual geometry, method and software bound. Recheck affected stock,
   contacts and every current neighbor after geometry changes.
8. Compare inside and outside views in fixed cameras. Retain failures and
   uncertain features. Authenticate operating direction and mechanism behaviour
   separately when the later animation phase begins.
