# Cylinder and head design review

Reviewed 2026-10-08 before feature planning. Target: GTSIO-520-H, four-ring piston reconstruction. Source: Continental X-30045A, original `../Notes/gtsio520_series.pdf`, SHA-256 `c8e7828e978772a55a9271d7bf678cc464ac772987ce7c44cfa3af89c9c21a07`. Source scan governs ambiguous OCR. This record extends the foundation scope; unbuilt systems remain deferred.

## Observed construction

- A-3-1 PDF16: aluminium heads are heated for valve seats/guides, then screwed and shrunk onto steel barrels. The installed head and barrel are permanent assemblies. Separate CAD identities describe material/construction, not a removable bolted joint. Covers are stamped sheet steel. Two inclined valves, inner/outer springs and split tapered keys are essential identities.
- A-4-17 PDF34, inspected complete figure and its 51-item legend: finned barrel, broad head fins, twin rocker covers, rocker shaft/bushing/retainer, two pushrods and housings, nested springs, exhaust rotocoil, separate guide/seat inserts and upper/lower spark-plug inserts. Four-ring configuration excludes the obsolete extra compression ring (35).
- A-4-23 PDF40, inspected section: intake axis 11 degrees 24 minutes to 11 degrees 36 minutes (11.5 degree midpoint); exhaust 14 degrees 54 minutes to 15 degrees 06 minutes (15 degree midpoint). Seat angles are 59 degrees 30 minutes to 60 degrees and 44 degrees 30 minutes to 45 degrees. The 5.75 in choke datum and 6.829-6.844 in dome **gauge** dimensions have distinct datums: neither is an overall cylinder length.
- Table 6 PDF103 / Table of limits PDF92: valve stem .4335-.4340 in (11.01725 mm midpoint); intake guide bore .4352-.4362 in (11.06678 mm), exhaust .4370-.4380 in (11.1125 mm). Rocker shaft .7490-.7495 in (19.03095 mm), rocker bush bore .7505-.7515 in (19.0754 mm). Stem length and seat sizes remain separately sourced in the controller.
- A-4-34 / 36 / 37 PDF46, visually reviewed: nested opposite-hand springs, spring/guide/seat construction, pivoted forged rocker and stamped cover; cylinder baffle is another identity, not a fin. Spring test lengths do not establish installed length.

## Engineering deductions and interfaces

The barrel carries piston/ring loads into the eight-position deck flange. Its pilot must enter the case aperture without treating the aluminium and steel as intersecting solids. The head sleeve surrounds the barrel with a neutral fitted boundary; the actual shrink interference and thread are simplified. Fin spacing and scallops must allow access to deck nuts. A common fitted bore controls barrel and rings. The lowest ring reaches about 79.5 mm from the crank axis at BDC, so the provisional open end is 76 mm; this supports future motion but does not constitute a motion audit.

Valve stem, guide bore and guide host seat are separate diameters. The valve face meets the conical insert; a crossing port communicates with its throat, and must not enter the spring cavity. A spring platform supports both coils, while the upper retainer and split keys transmit their force to the valve. The rocker bushing surrounds the shaft; its arm needs space above the retainer and a hollow cover. Casting unions and cavity cuts follow that assembly order.

## Explicit estimates

Initial packaging estimates: barrel outer radius 71 mm, open end 76 mm, flange 152-168 mm, barrel/head interface 256 mm, seat datum 270 mm, chamber roof 293 mm and valve offsets +/-33 mm. Later packaging checks below revise the open end to 94 mm. These are reconstruction estimates; **the dome gauge dimension has not been substituted for these datums**. Head cavity, thickness, outline, port and fin profiles remain estimates. The piston crown station is also estimated; no compression-ratio or combustion-volume claim is made. Source H compression ratio 7.5 remains a documented target for calibrated chamber reconstruction.

Head/cover envelopes are checked against the new 182 mm same-bank spacing. The valve axes diverge in the cylinder/shaft plane; rocker pivots are on their inward side to avoid crowding the neighbouring head. Exact clocking, lever arms, port routing and cam closure remain unverified until the full valve train is fitted. Installed springs use 35 mm envelopes, six turns, mean radii 15.5/10.5 mm, wires 2/1.4 mm; these are estimates, not manufacturer spring constants. Seats and guides use fitted neutral interfaces, not press-fit interference. Spark plug threads and barrel/head thread are simplified smooth interfaces.

## Native implementation and comparison plan

Reuse the installed FreeCAD 1.1.1 pipeline's checked revolution, polygon extrusion, linked ruled loft and helix sweep methods (legacy cylinder pilot and current Langley/Wright implementations). Revolutions express turned steel/aluminium sections; fin outlines use closed polygon extrusions; inclined cavities and rocker boxes use corresponding-section lofts; springs use separate native helix sweeps. FreeCAD remains the sole geometry master. Boolean cuts restore chambers and guest seats after casting additions.

Start with one cylinder and its actual deck, piston and neighbours. Check every final shape is a valid single solid, inspect all bound-meeting intersections, then expand to all six. Save/reopen native parts, test fitted-bore and guide parameter propagation, verify STEP and actual exported GLB. Render isolated cylinder, closed assembly and open head from repeatable cameras. Compare feature presence, negative space, fin silhouette and rocker-box construction with A-4-17 / A-4-23, separately from solid/interference acceptance. Retain substantive failures and resulting corrections. Pushrods, tappets, cam drive, baffles and complete external systems remain explicit backlog items until generated and checked.

### Helix method adoption

The installed Part::Helix exposes LocalCoord (Right-handed / Left-handed), rather than the PartDesign LeftHanded property. Native and direct sweeps now share an explicit handedness flag. Official method references: [Part Helix](https://github.com/FreeCAD/FreeCAD-documentation/blob/main/wiki/Part_Helix.md), [Part API](https://github.com/FreeCAD/FreeCAD-documentation/blob/main/wiki/Part_API.md). On FreeCAD 1.1.1 the disposable fixture saved/reopened opposite-hand coils, checked opposite winding, native/direct volume agreement below 1e-6, changed wire radius and verified the edited saved solid. One initial test sampled a complete turn instead of a quarter-turn; that test was corrected, without changing the geometry. Method dimensions are unrelated to manufacturer spring dimensions.

### Bank orientation and source correction

A-4-17 puts the exhaust flange below the cylinder; C-3-2 puts induction above. [EASA IM.E.248, Issue 01 (2022), PDF6](https://www.easa.europa.eu/sites/default/files/dfu/tcds_im_e248_gtsio520_20221108.pdf) independently describes top induction/bottom exhaust and lists H width 865 mm. The initial prototype tied port direction to the valve longitudinal offset and rotated the opposite bank about Z. That would invert its vertical systems. Revised construction rotates the opposite cylinder about gravity Y, keeps induction up/exhaust down and reverses its longitudinal valve positions. The estimated intake offset faces the paired cylinder; exact GTSIO-H lobe-to-follower stations remain to be checked. Shared intake cams in another Continental engine are a design clue, not substituted H geometry.

The first 82-part cylinder check passed 246 bound-meeting pairs with no overlaps after: crowned pin-plug ends to the pin-length envelope; separated keeper halves with a real split gap; cleared concentric spring platforms/retainers; shortened the estimated rocker tail to fit its box; extended screw pilot cuts; and provided spark-plug spotfaces through protruding fins. Four cover screws were replaced by five attaching sets per PDF80, with separate plain/lock washer envelopes. The first faceted-fin union failed at its third fin, despite a positive valid initial revolve. Annular fins with clearance flats resolved that kernel failure; reversed profile winding alone did not. Updated bank placement requires a fresh complete six-cylinder audit.

### Coupled estimate revision and local construction

The 839-part first full check retained 126 overlaps. The annular barrel addition needed its bore restored after the solid flange; its fourth-ring datum and large damper contour were incompatible with a continuous cylinder wall. Keeping the documented stroke/rod/race sizes, the selected damper crescent is now R85/R73 mm (outer loaded envelope 91.734 mm). Estimated oil/scraper stations are -17.5/-21.5 mm from the wrist, clear of the 14.280 mm pin-bore radius. At BDC the lowest ring edge is 94.725 mm; barrel open end 94 mm leaves 0.725 mm nominal axial support and 2.266 mm from the damper envelope. These are packaging estimates, not recovered dimensions or damper dynamics. The original R4 and 395-part foundation specifications are retained.

Deck positions at (+/-40,+/-66) mm left insufficient distance around the 71 mm barrel for washers/nuts. The new common deck/flange declaration moves them to (+/-42,+/-72), grows estimated flange halfspan to 80 mm, and leaves 1 mm stud projection beyond the nut (177.2 mm end), below the first fin at 178 mm. This resolves the layout cause rather than cutting arbitrary nut pockets into a thin steel wall.

Repeated placement exposed kernel failures when the same casting operations were evaluated after global transforms. Cylinder features now build in one local coordinate system and a single-solid Part::Compound links/places their final result. [Official compound documentation](https://github.com/FreeCAD/FreeCAD-documentation/blob/main/wiki/Part_Compound.md) warns about intersecting constituents: this wrapper contains exactly one solid. The saved/reopened fixture on FreeCAD 1.1.1 verified source-parameter propagation, translated/rotated bounds, native/direct volume agreement and STEP export. An initial antiparallel axis plus roll produced the wrong physical rotation; an explicit unit quaternion now fixes the gravity-Y bank rotation unambiguously. Longitudinal valve clocking remains an estimate: the original intake-negative/exhaust-positive local offsets are retained pending the complete cam/pushrod plan. Both global ports retain top induction and bottom exhaust.

### Rocker load-path review before valve-train expansion

A-4-36 PDF46 shows a lever reaching from the valve tip toward the pushrod cup below it. The first prototype placed the pivot shaft along vertical Y and swung the lever in the longitudinal X/Z plane. Although that can produce valid solids, it gives a straight pushrod from the below-crank camshaft an impossible route through the barrel. The source construction requires a downward lever tail and a pivot transverse to that lever. Planned correction: rocker lever in the valve-axis/vertical-Y plane; pivot shaft along the valve-plane transverse vector; vertical pivot offset -44 mm and cup -72 mm are estimates. An elongated rocker box surrounds the stem and lower cup. A straight pushrod to the lower cam tunnel then stays outside the steel barrel; the aluminium head receives its actual housing seat. No bent pushrod or arbitrary bore through the piston is acceptable. Exact lever ratio, cam law and follower stations remain unverified. Inspect A-4-17 and A-4-36 after the revised head reconstruction and re-run interfaces. The current R5 native job is retained as a static draft, not a valve-train fidelity acceptance.

The first R6 downward-rocker prototype passed: 82 positive single solids, 259 bound-meeting pairs and zero overlaps. This is a single-cylinder static result; shaft retention, straight pushrod seats, all six placements and native/export checks remain required.

### Straight-rod and spring checks

The corrected downward levers are coupled to a single skeleton in gtsio_valve_layout.py. Three intake follower stations are provisionally shared by opposed pairs; six exhaust stations are separate (nine source-supported lobes, assignment/stations still inferred). Mapping straight rods showed why longitudinal valve clocking matters: an inverted order makes the two housings cross. A native mirror of the already valid local construction corrects that clocking, followed by a proper gravity-Y bank rotation. This is a recorded geometry operation, separate from image registration. Raw coil winding is pre-reversed so the final coils retain opposite hands. The mirror/placement fixture saved/reopened, propagated a source edit and passed STEP/native/direct checks. Official method: [Part Mirror](https://github.com/FreeCAD/FreeCAD-documentation/blob/main/wiki/Part_Mirror.md).

Installed spring height was revised from an arbitrary 35 to 45 mm with the lower platform moved to retain the 110 mm top datum. Six turns of 4 mm outer wire cannot safely accommodate a 14 mm lift inside a 35 mm installed envelope; the revised estimated height provides positive pitch clearance. The installed height, wire, turns and 14 mm planned envelope remain illustrative. The 82-part prototype again passed 259 exact neighbouring pairs; standard-library checks confirm all twelve straight rod centre-lines clear the steel barrel/fin envelope and each other. Full solid housings, cam contact, shaft retention, native/export and operation are still unvalidated.

R5 full draft retained four piston/case intersections (about 1645.697 mm3 each). The case opening must clear the swept skirt at 69.475 mm, while the separate steel barrel begins at 94 mm and supports the lowest ring at 94.725 mm. The revised case opening starts at 69.275 mm, preserving main/cam bearing seats. R5 saved CAD built 839 valid solids / 3103 feature operations, but its STEP verifier failed (aggregate relative volume difference 0.00329247). That failure is retained and must be diagnosed; no tolerance was relaxed and no acceptance is claimed for that draft.

### Export and neighbouring-cover review

R5 STEP compound volume was 47,397,797.5 mm3, while its individual-solid sum was 47,218,016.2 mm3 and native sum 47,242,253.7 mm3. The six head solids differed by approximately 0.2987 percent. Both the aggregate and per-solid failures are retained; numerical integration is a hypothesis, not an exception or acceptance. Constant-section rocker boxes/levers are now native polygon extrusions, rather than unnecessary ruled lofts. This is a geometry-representation repair to test against strict saved/STEP checks.

R7 full placement found neighbouring rocker-cover intersections that the single-cylinder prototype could not detect. The estimated 4.5 mm cover depth and longitudinal side flats preserve 1.2 mm sheet thickness; screw/washer stations derive from the new top face. Gasket/casting positions and bank pitch remain unchanged. This matches the need for narrow bank-facing boundaries while retaining the vertical lever pocket. Repeat the six-head and complete assembly checks. The first opaque plate showed unexpected apparent openings, so source-face inspection and the revised extrusion tessellation are required before visual acceptance.

The six-cylinder cover/fastener subset now passes: 204 solids, 512 exact neighbouring pairs, no overlaps. The earlier flat-side iteration still overlapped because its circular screw pads projected beyond the flats; reducing the estimated cover/gasket pad radius to 3.5 mm cleared that cause while retaining the 4.5 mm cast anchor.

The extrusion fixture did not establish export acceptance: native head volume 1,332,674.2 mm3 versus direct 1,315,689.6 and STEP solid 1,332,186.7. Native/direct agreement also failed. Unrefined and sequential Boolean fixtures are running to distinguish topology/batching from integration. The STEP compound and its solid sum also differ. No comparator or tolerance has been changed.

Feature-by-feature comparison localised the first native/direct divergence to the intake crossing-port cut (feature 29): prior volumes agreed below 1e-12; the port result differed about 417 mm3. Fully sequential unrefined histories still differed about 1.2734 percent at the finished head. The exact cutter placements and geometric differences are being checked; batching/refinement alone did not repair it.

### Seam failures traced and corrected

The R11/R12 feature traces localized native/direct disagreement to intake-port and then spark-plug cutters ending exactly on the casting revolution seam at Y=0. Extending each internal cutter 1 mm past the seam, while retaining its external endpoint and diameter, resolved the discrepant topology. The complete saved sequential fixture agreed with the direct result within 3.44e-7 relative volume and with STEP within 2.36e-7; the batched fixture agreed within 3.45e-7 and 3.34e-7. Both contained 610 faces. This is a locally tested construction correction, not a recovered engine dimension or a validation-tolerance change.

Official implementation research used FreeCAD 1.1.1's FeaturePartBoolean.cpp, TopoShapePyImp.cpp, TopoShapeExpansion.cpp and FCBRepAlgoAPI_BooleanOperation.cpp from https://github.com/FreeCAD/FreeCAD/tree/1.1.1/src/Mod/Part/App . The native two-shape constructor computes automatic fuzzy tolerance; the direct operation uses a different setup. The trace and fixtures, rather than an assumed kernel explanation, establish the adopted correction. The old failed packages remain under build. A deferred-recompute experiment was interrupted after the source cutter errors were diagnosed; it was not adopted.

### Shared local geometry and export boundary

The six head recipes are identical in local coordinates, including their mirror. One editable local casting now drives six independent placed native compounds and stable part identities. The saved/reopened fixture checks source dimension edits, opposite-bank placement, distinct IDs and two STEP solids; all three placement tests passed. The direct evaluator independently rebuilds each recipe. Sharing is rejected if recipes, Boolean policies or mirror planes differ.

R13 exact development solids passed 2,859 neighbouring pairs without overlap, but its GLB failed because the right case had a three-edge T-junction along the split-face web/end seams. Finer deflection did not resolve it. FreeCAD seam refinement for tessellation reduced that case from 278 to 194 faces and produced a closed manifold; both Boolean differences were zero. The left counterpart failed the refinement boundary-preservation check, so it retains its already valid original mesh. R16's actual development export passed all 839 parts and 2,819 neighbouring pairs with no overlaps or waivers. Saved full-package checks remain distinct from these development results.

### Independent agreement-check correction

The completed saved-native audit found zero overlaps across 2,859 pairs, but its first scalar/bounds comparison retained 32 outliers. Six crowned pin plugs differed because the direct evaluator omitted the native Boolean refinement policy. Evaluating the same refinement restored their relative volume agreement to approximately 3e-11 to 3e-10 in the isolated probes; direct caches now distinguish the corrected evaluator.

The remaining keeper/bearing outliers had identical volumes to around 1e-13 relative and zero original-solid Boolean differences in both directions. Optimised bounding boxes varied by 0.0000102-0.0001633 mm for different analytic/location representations. Applying the same identity geometry conversion to both measurement representations reduced the bounding discrepancy to below 1e-12 mm. The original CAD solids and their volumes remain authoritative; the converted surfaces are used only for extrema measurement. The acceptance check retains the original 1e-5 mm bound threshold and additionally requires both original-solid differences below 1e-5 mm3 for these cases. No interference or STEP threshold was widened. The first failed agreement report is retained alongside the final result.
