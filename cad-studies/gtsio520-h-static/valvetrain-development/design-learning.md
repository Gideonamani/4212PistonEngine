No pixel score, global match percentage, manufactured cam claim or operating animation follows from these checks. Future work should calibrate forged rocker taper, housing end details and lifter axial proportions against stronger applicable references, then complete the reduction, accessory and external-system modules.

The hollow cup's first revolved reconstruction fixed STEP export but not assembly fit. Fusion left forged-arm material inside its void. The corrected feature history applies the bowl/mouth clearance to the full rocker, and the saved method fixture now includes socket/rod intersection alongside pad/stem contact. A valid hollow primitive is not proof that the completed host retains its cavity.

The reopened CPU fixture section also shows a more specific lifter discrepancy: its feed port is lower relative to the upper body, and its plunger reservoir has different axial proportions from A-4-10. Those levels must be calibrated together so the ports still connect the intended oil spaces. Preserve the follower face, socket datum and body-guide fit while revising the internal stack.
