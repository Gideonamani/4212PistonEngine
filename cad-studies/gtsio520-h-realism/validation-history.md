# Candidate validation history

7 October 2026. The first 103-solid, 566-feature candidate passed native solidity and a strict STEP round trip (aggregate volume difference approximately 1.03e-7, worst solid approximately 1.47e-6). Its exported development GLB **failed** the exhaustive static interference audit with 42 pairs. No overlap was accepted into a ledger and no threshold was changed.

The repeated conflicts were rod little-end eyes against undercrown ribs, full-length pin end plugs crossing the inherited provisional oil-ring station, bolt heads intruding into the rod forging, and nut envelopes crossing the cap contour. This demonstrates why inheriting a visually plausible single-cylinder layout is insufficient.

Repairs: move the estimated reinforcing ribs outboard of the actual little-end eye; place the estimated oil-ring station below the full-floating pin instead of crossing it; cut bolt-head and nut seats from their actual declared envelopes; derive rod width from the selected pin width minus the manual's .008 in side clearance, and narrow bolt-hole clearance to the documented limit's selected midpoint. Ring stations, rib dimensions and spotface contours remain estimates, not newly documented manufacturer measurements.

The failed development package remains locally under `build/gtsio520-core/`. Final checks and source hashes belong to the corrected package under `build/gtsio520-realism/`; consult its acceptance record before claiming a pass. No operating clip has been validated.

8 October: the first image-match revision added blades/bores but retained uniform extrusion depth. Design review corrected the blade/fork thickness relationship and the inclined cheek faces. Revision 3 uses thinner centred blades and ruled lofted webs with support lands; sizes remain estimates.

Two implementation failures were preserved during that change. Indexed native polygon-node expressions were stored but did not change geometry when the taper parameter changed; whole-vector-list expressions passed the reopened analytical-volume and direct-evaluator checks. Native automatic face refinement then emptied a loft/support-land fusion, with both batched and individual Booleans. Keeping refinement disabled for this forging produced a valid native solid; geometry, volume, STEP and interference checks retain their original limits. The diagnostic failed native file is local under `build/gtsio520-realism-r3/native-failure.FCStd`, and the successful one-part probe is explicitly incomplete. Only the full 103-part package can be accepted.
