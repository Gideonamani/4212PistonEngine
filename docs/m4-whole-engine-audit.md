# M4 whole-engine contract and export audit

Audit date: 12 September 2026. Asset audited: hash-bound `web/engine.glb` from the V5 Blender scene named in the engine contract.

## Export observation

The published GLB contains 1,822 nodes, 1,320 meshes, 1,819 animations and 5,457 animation channels. Its current hierarchy has 153 mesh-bearing names for each `C1` through `C6` prefix, 584 `V5` nodes and 284 `RUN | V3` nodes. It has zero node extras. This proves a balanced six-instance compatibility hierarchy and a present drivetrain/case representation; it does not prove dimensional equality, station datums, bearing mapping, gear ratios, or timing.

The current selector layer is therefore retained only as a contract-owned compatibility binding. The export script now emits `engine_id`, `module_id`, and `instance_id` extras without saving the Blender source. A newly exported GLB must be structurally checked, hash-bound into the contract, transported, browser-checked and released before the contract can leave `legacy-hierarchy-selector` mode.

## Claim boundary

Verified source/evidence-backed facts are the selected GTSIO-520-H study, the six-cylinder teaching scene, the stated firing order 1–4–5–2–3–6, the 720-degree export, and the documented V5 case check (two shells, 36 studs, eight half-webs and six through-bolts). The contract derives event phases 0, 120, 240, 360, 480 and 600 degrees in firing order.

The station labels, outward orientations, case/crank representation and drivetrain motion are teaching reconstruction or illustrative behavior as classified in the contract. Unknowns deliberately left open are applicable manual mounting datums and bank angles, journal-to-bearing mapping, crank-throw indexing, primary-drive ratios, and whole-engine valve/gas timing. The detailed single-cylinder valve and gas lesson is not evidence for those six full-engine instances.

## M4/M5 boundary

M4 provides the reusable instance and interface foundation. M5 can add a connected subsystem only by attaching it to the contract's mounting, crankshaft-to-rod, or crankshaft-to-drive interfaces and by adding its own evidence classification. It must not reproduce cylinder placement, phase or firing logic in a separate web or Blender implementation.
