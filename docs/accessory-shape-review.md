# Manual illustration / model shape audit — 3 October 2026

The first 47-part release was a relationship schematic. Its rectangular box, auxiliary inspection lid and generic cylindrical bodies did **not** represent the actual accessory section faithfully. This revision reconstructs visible features from the reviewed GTSIO manual and removes the invented lid. It is an **unscaled teaching reconstruction**, not a dimensional replica or a fully faithful complete engine installation.

The source PDF and SHA-256 are in `accessory-reference-ledger.json`. PDF page numbers below are one-based. Illustrations were viewed as page images, including the H photograph, and read with their captions. H applicability comes from C-3-1 (PDF 151); the basic chapter describes a hypothetical basic GTSIO configuration, so the parts drawings establish shared forms rather than every H production detail. No IO-520 geometry is substituted.

Each selectable solid has `Evidence`, `DimensionStatus`, `ShapeStatus` and `Function` properties in FreeCAD. Blender preserves the same evidence as object custom properties; the web inspector displays the form status. Drawings support visible traits; the added depth, underside, openings and thickness of reconstructed solids remain model assumptions. Section caps preserve the **model's** cavities, not a claim to have recovered every real casting gallery.

Before replacing the source, the previous native assembly, contract and export were copied to `.local/accessory-before-shape-review`. New native and web releases are versioned in Drive; the previous working Drive files are retained. Git receives scripts, contracts, lesson content, this audit and validation records only.

Validation includes visual review of isolated starter/oil/alternator renders against the pages above, native solid and STEP reopening, native action-slot reopening, exported motion/path/section checks, the existing lesson/model suite and browser checks. No pixel-matching test can establish engineering accuracy from these unscaled perspective illustrations. Physical comparison with a GTSIO-520-H trainer, measured dimensions, serial-specific parts identification and instructor moderation remain outstanding.

## Connected assembly revision

The 64-solid drawing-led release is preserved in `.local/accessory-before-connected-review-20261003` and its versioned Drive files. The new assembly contains 80 engine/interface part studies and three author-created teaching fixtures. These counts are study objects, not a manufacturer bill of materials.

- Lobed rear casting and raised mounting pads now meet their gaskets and flanges. Case fastener shanks enter bored seats; the idler pin reaches the case wall. Contour and pad positions remain inferred.
- Fuel gear shaft, detachable coupling, pump input tang and locating sleeve form a continuous input path. The pump gasket now meets the pump body. The added sleeve is an illustrative interface, not a positively identified manufacturer part.
- Oil/tach shaft enters the cam gear's internal spline socket and reaches the pump driver and bevel drive. The driven pump gear has a fixed support pin; the oil casing sits left of the shaft to clear the fuel bay. Relief housing and plug meet their casting ports.
- Tach output turns through the documented 90-degree bevel pair to the lateral pad. Smooth pitch cones demonstrate the connection without inventing manufactured teeth. Their signed speeds produce matching tangential velocity at the study contact point.
- Starter case bushing, drum shaft, wheel hub and motor/worm input now share seated interfaces. The adapter has a wheel chamber, open cross-axis worm tunnel and supporting cover ears. The worm reaches the motor input. The adapter wall rises to the worm tunnel's crest so the gasket, cover and seal seat on it instead of crossing the tunnel, and the worm sits 0.3 mm off the wheel's tooth tips; a true worm mesh needs a throated wheel, which this study does not model.
- Alternator gasket, body flange, gear cup, bushing, thrust washer, clutch sleeve and hub form a connected separate module. A-4-13 item 5 was previously mislabelled as a mounting pad; it is now correctly identified as the gasket.
- Three grey display stands support the relocated alternator, vacuum and governor studies. They are fixed teaching fixtures, excluded from torque edges, and do not imply an engine mounting or transmission. Missing front/optional transfers remain dashed conceptual routes.

FreeCAD reopening checks declared mounting/shaft interfaces and selected interference pairs against the actual native solids. These are consistency checks on this reconstruction; the recorded model gaps are not approved engineering clearances. Section cavities remain actual voids. Blender retains ten named motions and every component slot. Web path anchors follow visible geometry centres rather than arbitrary object origins, including during explosion.

CW/CCW remains **facing each engine drive pad**, as specified in H C-3-3. The magneto front-pad direction and lateral tach axis are represented explicitly. Crank/intermediate signed rotations are inferred from external gear meshes and the front magneto viewpoint, not quoted as a separate manufacturer crank-rotation specification.
